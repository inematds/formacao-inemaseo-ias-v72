Lesson 3 · 18 min

Measure citations without an API: the frozen question battery

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity have no dashboard for the site owner. The honest way to measure is a repeated sample: the same questions, in the same AIs, every month, with a date. Because answers change with every run, you measure an appearance rate, not a snapshot.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Write neutral questions (without your brand) and branded ones
  2. 2Freeze the battery so the monthly comparison doesn't break
  3. 3Run it by hand, 3 times, and log brand, sources, and errors
  4. 4Why a local model and a paid tool don't replace this

Goal: build a battery of 20 frozen questions and run it by hand on 4 assistants, logging everything in a dated spreadsheet.

01 · From the question to the appearance rate

Click each block.

every month, the same questions
Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

A repeated, dated sample: that's what makes the number comparable month to month.

02 · Set up and run

Tap each step.

1. Write 20 questions

List the real questions with people who know the audience: the non-branded queries from Search Console, customer questions, the grounding queries from Bing (lesson 2).

Write the way a person would write to an AI, in a full sentence.

2. Freeze it with a date

Save the list as bateria-v1.md with the date and the rule "this battery does not change." A new question goes into a v2.

3. Run it 3 times

For each assistant, paste the question, read the answer and the sources, and fill in the row. Repeat 3 times: the answer changes, and that variation is what the rate absorbs.

With 20 questions, 4 assistants, and 3 runs, that's 240 rows. You can split it across days; what matters is recording the date.

A shortcut that doesn't workRunning the questions on a local model (Ollama). It doesn't have the search, the index, or the weights of the real AIs: it doesn't measure what they answer.

4. Calculate and compare

% of appearance in the neutral questions per assistant, the 5 most-cited sources in your place, and the factual errors about you. Compare only with the same battery and the same assistants.

A new assistant becomes a new column; never swap one for another.

Example from the repository

A single round on 07/10/2026, battery v1 with 20 questions: INEMA appeared in 0 of 48 neutral answers on Claude and 0 of 4 on Gemini (52 neutral measurements). ChatGPT and Perplexity were not measured.

"O que é INEMA?" got 4 of 4 answers about a different organization with the same name. That's the topic of lesson 4.

The July round used an API, through a key authorized at that time. This course does not repeat that path: by hand, through the account you already use, no robot.

An answer from an API can differ from what a person sees in the app, and a paid "share of voice" tool is also a sample of prompts, not an official score.

04 · Practice: your battery v1

E2 takes a few hours; split it across days if you need to.

E1 · Frozen battery

40 min

Write the 20 questions (16 neutral, 4 branded) for your topic, using the non-branded queries from Search Console and the grounding queries from lesson 2. Save it with the date and the freeze rule.

Done when: bateria-v1.md exists, with a date, the "does not change" rule, 16 neutral questions without the brand, and 4 branded ones.

E2 · First round

3 h

Run the battery on 4 assistants, 3 times each, by hand, and fill in medicoes.csv. At the end, calculate the % of appearance in the neutral questions per assistant and list the 5 most-cited sources in your place.

Done when: medicoes.csv has the rows from the round with date and assistant, and a summary with the % per assistant and the 5 most-cited sources.

06 · Takeaway

Repeated sample20 frozen questions, 4 to 5 assistants, 3 runs, every month, with a date.
What to look atAppearance rate (%) on the neutral questions, sources cited in your place, and errors about you.
2026 yardstickNobody has the official score from the AIs: a local model doesn't measure it, and a paid tool is a sample.
