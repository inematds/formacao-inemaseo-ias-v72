AI readiness rubric and its caveats

An AI readiness score helps you find what's broken, as long as you know how it's calculated. No score is official: each scanner picks its own criteria and weights. Here you compare two open INEMA rubrics, read what weighs most, and learn what the score does not prove.

02 · The two rubrics and the caveats

Click each tab.

1. WebMCP Readiness

The INEMA scanner adds up four blocks: WebMCP 30%, SEO 30%, GEO 20%, and AEO 20%. Each finding is worth pass, warning, or fail; an informational-only finding is worth 0.7.

GEO looks at entity, substantial content (250+ words), authorship and date, sources, and semantic HTML. AEO looks at questions in headings, direct answers, lists, and tables.

2. Caveat 1

In Readiness, a site without WebMCP gets a low overall score even with perfect SEO, because that block weighs 30%. WebMCP is a draft with no stable browser (lesson 7): read that block as preparation.

3. Caveat 2

AEO criteria reward headings phrased as questions with a short answer right after. That is clarity for the reader, not an AI technique with a source behind it. And the FAQ rich result stopped showing in Google on 05/07/2026: FAQPage markup no longer gets you a showcase.

Don't create artificial questions just to score points.

4. agente-ready

Open evolution of Readiness: weights in a versioned file, with the reason and source for each criterion; WebMCP weighs 8 out of 100; llms.txt weighs 2.

Limits: sample of home + 4 pages, CDN test by User-Agent only, WebMCP detected only in static code.

03 · Run it and read the score

agente-ready only reads public pages, with no API (detailed in course 7). WebMCP Readiness (webmcp.inema.pro) opens the page in a disposable browser and goes deeper only on the URL you provide.

agente-ready (inemaseo-sistemas, MIT)
git clone https://github.com/inematds/inemaseo-sistemas && cd inemaseo-sistemas/ferramentas/agente-ready
python3 agente-ready.py https://seusite.com --saida saida/seusite
# output: relatorio.html, agente-ready.json and criterios.csv
# the weights live in pesos.json: disagree, change them and run again

Example from the repository

agente-ready 1.0.0 on INEMA sites, home + 4 pages:

site                         nota  entidade  conteudo  descoberta  webmcp
inema.club                   93    86        81        100         100
news.inema.pro               72    0         71        100         29
eventos.inema.pro            69    0         62        100         29
guia REA (github.io)         54    0         62        17          29
  • inema.club: loses points only for the About/Contact link on the home page and for partial authorship and date.
  • news and eventos: entity scores zero (no Organization/Person with @id on the home page); this is the heaviest fix.
  • guia REA: no sitemap, no lastmod and no canonical; discovery drops to 17.
  • Bot access: 100 on all four.

None of these scores tells you whether the site is cited. inema.club at 93 and the July measurement of 0 out of 52 neutral citations coexist: readiness is a condition, citation is a result.

06 · Takeaway

A score is a rubricEach scanner picks its own criteria and weights; prefer public weights.
Prioritize by weightBot access, content in the HTML, and entity come before details.
2026 yardstickA high score is not a citation. WebMCP and questions in headings are preparation and clarity, not a ranking factor.
